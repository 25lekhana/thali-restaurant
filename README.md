# Tasty Bites Restaurant Website

A beginner-friendly restaurant website made with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## Files
- `index.html` — website content
- `style.css` — colours, layout and design
- `script.js` — menu cards and category filter

## How to see it on your computer
1. Download all three files.
2. Keep them together in one folder.
3. Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser.
4. Internet is needed for the food photos.

## How to upload it to GitHub
1. Sign in at https://github.com
2. Click the **+** button near the top-right, then **New repository**.
3. Name it `tasty-bites-restaurant`.
4. Choose **Public**, then click **Create repository**.
5. Click **Add file** → **Upload files**.
6. Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, and `README.md` (not the ZIP file).
7. Click **Commit changes**.
8. To publish the website, open **Settings** → **Pages**.
9. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
10. Choose branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
11. Wait a little, then refresh the Pages section. GitHub will show your website link there.

The Add buttons are only a demo and do not process real orders. Replace the sample contact details with your own before publishing.
